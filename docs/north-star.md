# North star

A living fantasy world that runs on its own logic, where you can exist and play a role.

Every system in an upgrade should serve at least one of these. If it serves none, it does not ship.

1. **Mundane first.** Supply, tithe, mills, aging, and rumor work with no magic. Magic is a layer on top of that logic.
2. **No dead ends.** A good, a person, a road, and a rumor each lead somewhere else.
3. **No ending.** Land, a town, knighthood, and a crown are goals. The world continues, and Mori keeps shaping it.
4. **Role play is the spark.** Try anything the world's law allows. Rules do the math. Flavor does the feel. Effects stay capped and light-hearted.
5. **Uneven on purpose.** Regions, races, and tribes differ in tech, magic, and resources. Trade, teaching, and travel make hybrids.
6. **Built with Mori.** She is the guide and a co-builder, not a menu.
7. **AAA feel, phone weight.** Original code. It has to run on a phone.

Parkour (Dying Light) stays on the backlog. It is designed in `docs/inspirations.md` and is not part of this build.
